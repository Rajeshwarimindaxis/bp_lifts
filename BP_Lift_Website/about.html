Brochure Safety Standards

Core Safety Features Engineered Into Every BP Lift

Passenger safety is paramount. Every BP Lift includes comprehensive mechanical, electrical, and audio-visual safeguards.

Infrared Light Curtain Full-height optical door safety beam that prevents closing on passengers.
Overload Sensor with Audio-Visual Indication Stops elevator from moving if weight limit is breached and alerts riders.
Emergency Alarm & Push To Talk Hands-free two-way intercom for instant rescue communication.
Voice Annunciator with Music Comforting audio floor notifications, travel guidance, and background music.
Emergency Rescue Button Allows passengers to trigger immediate rescue protocols in unforeseen halts.
Safety in Case of Over Travel Terminal limit switches and hydraulic/spring buffers preventing overtravel.
Safety in Case of Rope Breakage Progressive mechanical safety clamps that grip the guide rails securely.
PLC Based Controller with V3F Variable frequency drive delivering smooth start, leveling, and stop.
