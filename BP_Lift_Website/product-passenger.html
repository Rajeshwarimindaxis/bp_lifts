Core Technology

Precision Engineering Built for Daily Traffic

Gearless PMSM Efficiency

Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors consume up to 40% less electrical power than conventional geared drives, requiring zero motor gear oil and generating negligible heat.

📐 Civil Shaft & Structural Guidelines

  • ✓ Vertical Plumb Shaft: The lift hoistway must be true and vertical with maximum plumb deviation within +/- 25mm over entire height.
  • ✓ Waterproof Pit: Pit base must be 100% waterproof concrete, dry, clean, and capable of withstanding dynamic buffer impact reactions.
  • ✓ RCC Machine Room / MRL Wall: Hoistway walls must be solid concrete or RCC lintel beams at 2000mm vertical intervals for bracket fastening.
  • ✓ Ventilation Louvers: Provide adequate ventilation openings at the top of the shaft (minimum 1% of shaft cross-sectional area).
Architectural Assistance: Our engineering team provides detailed 2D AutoCAD shaft layout drawings and structural reaction load schedules upon site review.

🛡️ Multi-Layer Passenger Safety Standards

  • ✓ Automatic Rescue Device (ARD): Dedicated dry-battery backup system automatically brings the cab to nearest landing in power outages.
  • ✓ Full-Height Infrared Curtain: 128-beam light curtain detects passengers or objects before door contact, reopening instantly.
  • ✓ Bi-directional Overspeed Governor: Mechanical safety gear wedges car firmly to guide rails if downward speed exceeds limits.
  • ✓ Hands-Free Intercom & Alarm: Emergency passenger communication linked directly to building security and BP LIFTS 24/7 squad.
Government Certification: All installations are certified by the Government Electrical Inspectorate / Lift Inspector Pune before commissioning.
