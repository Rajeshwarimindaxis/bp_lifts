Modernization / Upgradation

Our professional staff has a wealth of experience in all types of elevator modernizations. A Modernization could mean replacing outdated control equipment, updating your cab interior to a more modern look, or a complete system replacement.

  • ✓ Upgrade old relay panels to cutting-edge energy-saving PLC & V3F drive controllers for silky smooth rides.
  • ✓ Complete cabin makeover: hairline stainless steel, panoramic glass, designer false ceilings, and ambient LED lighting.
  • ✓ Retrofit modern safety systems: infrared multi-beam door curtains, overload sensors, and voice annunciators.
  • ✓ Significant reduction in energy bills and eliminating recurrent breakdown costs.
