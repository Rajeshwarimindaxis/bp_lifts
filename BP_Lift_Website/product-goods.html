📐 Civil Shaft & Structural Guidelines

  • ✓ Structural Tie Beams: Concrete lintel tie beams must be installed at intervals not exceeding 1.8 meters to anchor heavy-duty guide brackets.
  • ✓ High Pit Load Bearing: Pit foundation slab must be reinforced to withstand dynamic impact buffer reactions under full capacity load drops.
  • ✓ Hoistway Enclosure: Full masonry brickwork or 150mm thick RCC shaft wall enclosure to eliminate acoustic vibration and dust infiltration.
  • ✓ Overhead Hoisting Beam: A structural I-beam (ISMB) must be cast into the top of the machine room for motor rigging and rope installation.
Engineering Support: BP LIFTS provides structural load reaction charts and civil foundation layouts tailored to your structural consultant's drawings.

🛡️ Heavy-Duty Industrial Safety Gear

  • ✓ Progressive Safety Arrestor: Hardened steel wedge cams grip the 16mm/19mm guide rails smoothly to arrest the car within controlled deceleration limits.
  • ✓ Electromechanical Gate Interlocks: Dual-redundant electrical contacts ensure the hoistway gate cannot be unlocked while the car is in motion.
  • ✓ Slack Rope Safety Switch: Automatically cuts off power to the drive motor if tension on any hoisting rope falls below safety parameters.
  • ✓ Phase Failure & Inversion Protection: Prevents motor reverse rotation in case of Phase sequence alterations from the main grid.
AMC & Factory Compliance: Complete Form 11 inspection certification and quarterly preventative AMC to keep your factory compliant with the Maharashtra Factories Act.
