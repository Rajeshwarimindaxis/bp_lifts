Residential Engineering

Bespoke Vertical Living Crafted for Your Home

Every BP LIFTS home elevator is engineered to seamlessly blend into your existing interior architecture, without complex structural modifications or high electricity bills.

🔌

Runs on Single Phase 230V

Connects directly to your regular home electrical meter (single-phase 230V, 50Hz). Consumes less energy than a typical home air conditioner while in operation.

🏡

Zero Pit & No Machine Room

Can be installed on existing finished flooring with as little as 100mm to 150mm ramp or zero pit, eliminating deep digging or water seepage concerns.

🤫

Whisper-Quiet Operation

Advanced synchronous drive with acoustic dampening guarantees silent operation under 45 dB, ensuring your family sleeps peacefully even when in use.

🔋

Built-In Power Failure ARD

Equipped with an automatic emergency rescue battery. If Pune grid power cuts out, the lift gently travels to the nearest floor, opens the door, and illuminates the cabin.
