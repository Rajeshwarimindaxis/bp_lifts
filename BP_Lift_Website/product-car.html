Car Elevators: Safe & High-Tonnage Vehicle Transit

Designed for luxury multi-story residential garages, car dealership showrooms, and commercial multi-tier parking systems in Pune. Built with reinforced wide-gauge steel platforms capable of supporting modern electric SUVs and luxury sedans with drive-through walk-way gates and optical positioning guides.

Payload Capacity 3,000 kg – 5,000 kg

Automotive Engineering

Built to Manage Heavy Dynamic Wheel Loads

Unlike standard cargo, automobiles present dynamic concentrated tire loads when entering a lift car. BP LIFTS vehicle elevators are structurally reinforced to handle heavy eccentric axle weights smoothly.

🚗

Heavy Cantilever Steel Chassis

Constructed with dual-side structural uprights and cross-braced steel channels to absorb uneven axle loads as vehicles enter the platform.

🚦

Traffic Control LED Signals

Exterior visual green/red signal lights and sound chimes guide the driver inside when the car is perfectly level and gates are fully retracted.

🛑

Integrated Tire Guide Curbs

Heavy steel side curbs and floor wheel stoppers prevent vehicle tires from drifting or scraping against the elevator car interior walls.

🔄

Walk-Through Front & Rear Doors

Dual-door configurations allow the driver to drive in from one side and drive out forward on the destination parking floor without reversing.

Smart Vehicle Systems

Equipped with Modern Driver Convenience Technology

Everything is designed to make vehicle transit effortless, safe, and intuitive for residential drivers and valet parking attendants.

📱

Remote Radio Control (RF)

Wireless dashboard remote transmitter allows drivers to call the elevator from up to 20 meters away on approach.

👁️

Optoelectronic Car Detectors

Multi-beam optical sensors detect vehicle bumpers, preventing doors from closing while any part of the vehicle overhangs.

🛞

Wheel Centering Chocks

Raised guide rails along the platform edges assist the driver in centering the automobile onto the platform perfectly.

💡

High-Lumen Car Floodlights

Bright overhead LED floodlights and floor marker strips ensure crisp visibility in dark basement garages.

📐 Structural Shaft & Driveway Planning

  • ✓ Driveway Turning Radius: Ensure a minimum 5.5-meter approach radius leading to the elevator portal for smooth car entry.
  • ✓ Heavy Pit Load Support: Foundation must support combined car, counterweight, and 5-tonne live wheel loads.
  • ✓ Ventilation & Exhaust: Provide exhaust air ducting to remove carbon monoxide fumes during vehicle idle time.
  • ✓ Drainage & Sump Pump: Include an automatic pit sump pump to catch rain water carried in on automobile tires.
Parking Planning Consultation: We coordinate directly with your architect to optimize ramp-free multi-tier parking space inside your building.

🛡️ Multi-Point Automobile Safety Protection

  • ✓ Anti-Creep Leveling System: Hydraulic models include automatic re-leveling valves to keep the floor flush as weight shifts.
  • ✓ Dual Mechanical Safety Gear: Progressive safety gear clamps instantaneously if hoisting rope tension or speed is compromised.
  • ✓ Rupture Valve Safety: Hydraulic circuits feature rapid burst valves that lock the car in place in case of pressure drop.
  • ✓ Intercom & CCTV Integration: In-cabin camera mounting points and speakerphone connecting directly to society security.
Maharashtra Lift Inspector Certified: All BP LIFTS vehicle elevators undergo strict load testing and government licensing before handover.
