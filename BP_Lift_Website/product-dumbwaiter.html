Dumbwaiter Elevators: Hygienic & Compact Vertical Transit

Precision small-freight vertical service elevators engineered specifically for restaurants, multi-story commercial kitchens, hospital pathology labs, pharmaceutical dispensaries, hotels, and luxury villas. Built with certified SS 304 food-grade stainless steel cabins, space-saving vertical bi-parting doors, and multi-tier adjustable shelving to eliminate stair fatigue.

Payload Capacity 50 kg – 250 kg
Cabin Material Food-Grade SS 304
Door Mechanism Vertical Bi-Parting
▲ Food & Hospital Grade SS 304 IS 14665 Compliance & Pune Lift Act Certified
Core Engineering

Designed for Fast, Sanitary & Effortless Floor-to-Floor Dispatch

Whether sending multi-course meals from a basement kitchen to a terrace lounge or transferring sterile lab samples across diagnostic floors, BP LIFTS dumbwaiters provide whisper-quiet, zero-spill operation.

🍲

SS 304 Food-Grade Hygiene

Constructed from non-porous hairline stainless steel with seamless rolled edges, preventing bacterial harboring and allowing rapid wipe-down sanitization between kitchen orders.

↕️

Vertical Bi-Parting Doors

Counterbalanced vertical bi-parting shutters open up and down smoothly within the shaft frame, requiring zero corridor swing clearance and preserving tight kitchen space.

🛎️

Micro-Controller "Call & Send"

Intuitive push-button consoles at each landing allow staff to dispatch the carriage to any level or call it back, accompanied by dual-tone arrival chimes and illuminated in-use indicators.

🍱

Adjustable Multi-Tier Shelving

Includes removable stainless steel perforated trays and spill lip retainers, easily reconfigured to accommodate tall banquet soup pots, bakery trays, or medical specimen boxes.

Architectural Integration

Civil Shaft & Structural Planning Guidelines

Designed with minimal civil overhead, zero deep pit requirements, and available with free-standing self-supporting steel structures for retrofits.

🍽️

Counter-Height or Floor Loading

Configurable for counter-level sill (750 mm above finished floor) for effortless dish loading or floor-level flush sill for roll-in food and laundry trolleys.

🏗️

Zero Pit & Minimal Headroom

Requires only 2400 mm to 2800 mm overhead clearance at the top floor. No floor depression or pit excavation is needed for counter-height models.

🔩

Self-Supporting Steel Tower

If your property lacks a masonry shaft, we supply an engineered structural steel tower that bolts directly between floor slabs with minimal disruption.

⚡

Single-Phase & 3-Phase Power

Compact energy-efficient drum or traction hoist machines operate on standard household 230V AC or 415V 3-phase supply, consuming minimal power.

Tailored Accessories

Specialized Features for Hospitality, Medical & Luxury Residences

Enhance operational workflow with specialized configurations built for specific commercial demands.

♨️

Thermostatic Heating Chamber

Optional insulated cabin with internal heating elements keeps banquet meals, sizzlers, and coffee piping hot during vertical transport to rooftop dining areas.

🏥

Pharma & Lab Sterile Finishes

Electro-polished Grade 316 stainless steel interiors and airtight silicone gaskets for cleanrooms, pathology labs, and hospital blood banks.

🪟

Toughened Vision Panels

Landing doors can be fitted with fire-resistant tempered glass vision strips so kitchen and service staff can verify car arrival without opening the hatch.

🔐

Key-Switch & PIN Access

Electronic key locks or access pins to restrict access to dumbwaiter carriages carrying premium bar liquors, medical narcotics, or confidential documents.

Zero Compromise

Multi-Redundant Safety & Interlock Protections

Every BP LIFTS dumbwaiter incorporates industrial-grade safety circuits complying with the Maharashtra Lift Act and IS 14665.

🔒

Dual Mechanical & Electrical Interlocks

Landing vertical doors are mechanically locked until the car has arrived and leveled within +/- 3mm. The lift will not run if any door is open or ajar.

🛑

Slack Rope & Safety Cam Clamp

Instant mechanical safety clamp engages the guide rails immediately if cable tension drops or rope slack is detected, halting the cabin safely.

🔔

Arrival Chimes & In-Use Visual Signals

Audible buzzer announces car arrival at the targeted landing while bright LED signals warn staff on other floors that the lift is currently in transit.

⚡

Low-Voltage 24V Control Circuit

All external push buttons, indicator fixtures, and door safety interlocks operate on safe 24V DC to safeguard kitchen and cleaning personnel.

🎛️

Thermal Overload & Phase Reversal Protection

Solid-state micro-controller monitors motor temperature and supply voltage phases, preventing motor burnout during continuous rush-hour kitchen cycles.

🛠️

Terminal Limit Switches & Spring Buffers

Independent upper and lower over-travel limit switches with heavy-duty energy accumulation spring buffers prevent shaft overrun.
