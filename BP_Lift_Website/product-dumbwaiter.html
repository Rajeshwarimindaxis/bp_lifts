Core Engineering

Designed for Fast, Sanitary & Effortless Floor-to-Floor Dispatch

Whether sending multi-course meals from a basement kitchen to a terrace lounge or transferring sterile lab samples across diagnostic floors, BP LIFTS dumbwaiters provide whisper-quiet, zero-spill operation.

📐 Civil Shaft & Structural Planning Guidelines

  • ✓ Zero Pit Requirement: Counter-height models require zero pit excavation or floor depression, making retrofit installation seamless in existing properties.
  • ✓ Minimal Overhead Clearance: Requires only 2400 mm to 2800 mm overhead clearance at the top floor for drive machine mounting.
  • ✓ Self-Supporting Steel Tower: If your property lacks a masonry shaft, we supply an engineered structural steel tower that bolts directly between floor slabs.
  • ✓ Single-Phase & 3-Phase Power: Compact energy-efficient drum or traction hoist machines operate on standard household 230V AC or 415V 3-phase supply.
Architectural Assistance: Our engineering team provides detailed 2D AutoCAD shaft layout drawings, wall aperture dimensions, and electrical load schedules upon site review.

🛡️ Food, Medical & Mechanical Safety Standards

  • ✓ Dual Mechanical & Electrical Interlocks: Landing doors are mechanically locked until the car has arrived and leveled; the lift will not run if any door is ajar.
  • ✓ Slack Rope & Safety Cam Clamp: Instant mechanical safety clamp engages the guide rails immediately if cable tension drops or rope slack is detected.
  • ✓ Low-Voltage 24V Control Circuit: All external push buttons, indicator fixtures, and door safety interlocks operate on safe 24V DC to protect staff.
  • ✓ Thermal Overload & Phase Protection: Solid-state controller monitors motor temperature and supply phases, preventing burnout during continuous rush-hour cycles.
Government Certification: Certified to Indian Standard IS 14665 and Maharashtra Lift Act standards with 100% component safety testing before commissioning.
